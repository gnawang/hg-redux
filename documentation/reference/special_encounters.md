Every area has 10 equal slots, each 10%. Evening uses the day table, and late night uses the night table.

Bonus Pokémon are locked until the area has reached the listed levels of Plains, Forest, Peak or Water objects. Each unlocked bonus takes one of the 10 slots, so it's a 10% chance.
