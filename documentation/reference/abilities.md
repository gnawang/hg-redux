- Pokémon: Caterpie, Weedle, Venomoth, Wurmple, Dustox
- Battle rules: Mold Breaker, Teravolt and Turboblaze ignore it

## Simple

- Description: The Pokémon is prone to wild stat changes.
- Pokémon: Numel, Bidoof, Bibarel
- Battle rules: Mold Breaker, Teravolt and Turboblaze ignore it

## Skill Link

- Description: Increases the frequency of multi-strike moves.
- Pokémon: Shellder, Cloyster
- Hidden ability of: Aipom, Ambipom

## Slow Start

- Description: Temporarily halves Attack and Speed.
- Pokémon: Regigigas

## Sniper

- Description: Powers up moves if they become critical hits.
- Pokémon: Horsea, Seadra, Remoraid, Octillery, Kingdra, Skorupi, Drapion
- Hidden ability of: Beedrill, Spearow, Fearow, Spinarak, Ariados

## Snow Cloak

- Description: Raises evasion in a hailstorm.
- Pokémon: Swinub, Piloswine, Glaceon, Mamoswine, Froslass
- Hidden ability of: Articuno
- Battle rules: Mold Breaker, Teravolt and Turboblaze ignore it
- Note: Works in snow as well as hail.

## Snow Warning

- Description: The Pokémon summons a hailstorm in battle.
- Pokémon: Snover, Abomasnow
- Note: Summons snow, not hail. Snow does no damage and raises Ice-types' Defense by half.

## Solar Power

## Solid Rock

## Suction Cups

- Description: Negates foes’ moves that force switching out.

- Battle rules: Mold Breaker, Teravolt and Turboblaze ignore it
- Note: Out of battle, a lead Pokémon with it adds 5% to the chance of finding an item in a smashed rock.
